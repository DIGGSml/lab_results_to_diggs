Lab Results → DIGGS

Turn laboratory soil test results into a DIGGS 3.0 XML file. Everything below works offline on your computer. Nothing is uploaded unless you press one of the optional online buttons.

1Fill in the workbook

The Excel workbook has a sheet for the project, the borings, the samples and each test. Every results row names the boring, the sample and its depth, so results on different sheets join up by boring_id + sample_id. Fill in only the sheets for the tests you ran.

The workbook is created by this page. It opens in Excel, LibreOffice and Google Sheets; keep it as .xlsx when you save.

What goes on each sheet
SheetMethodColumns (* required)
Prefer CSV files?

Each sheet can also be supplied as its own CSV file with the same header row. Name the file after the sheet (for example gradation.csv) or keep the headers, and the page recognises it.

2Project and units

Filled in from the workbook's Project sheet when you add it. You can change them here.

Values are written exactly as entered, labelled with these units. Water content, limits, percent passing and organic content are percent. Particle sizes are mm. Resistivity is ohm-cm. Sulfate and chloride are ppm.

3Add your filled-in workbook

Drop your workbook here or click to choose it .xlsx workbooks and .csv files are read on this computer. Nothing is uploaded.

4Boring locations

DIGGS needs a latitude and longitude (WGS84 decimal degrees) for every boring. Ground elevation is strongly recommended. Values from the Borings sheet are filled in; you can type or correct them here.
